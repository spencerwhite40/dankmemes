Under the hood

It doesn't guess. It watches.

A reaction meme works when the clip matches the feeling, not the topic. So every run goes through four passes.

Pass 01

Watch

A video model watches the entire thing and flags moments that show one clear, reusable feeling.

Pass 02

Write

Your audience's profile writes three candidate captions per moment, pulled from real situations in their life.

Pass 03

Check

The model re-watches each moment, trims it, and keeps the caption it lands best. Forced matches get dropped.

Pass 04

Render

Cut on pauses, framed 9:16 over a blurred fill, caption burned in, audio levelled. 1080×1920.

FAQ

Questions, answered

What kind of video works best?

People talking and reacting: podcasts, interviews, streams, panels, reality TV you have the rights to. The more faces and feelings, the more memes. Silent videos work, but the clips will be silent too.

How many memes do I get from one video?

It depends on how many clean moments the video has. Every plan has a cap per run: 12 on Free, up to 60 on the bigger plans. Weak pairings are dropped, so you only get clips that actually land.

Is there a watermark?

Free and Starter clips carry a small "made with dankmemes" mark burned into the picture. Creator and up are clean.

Who is it for?

Anyone with an audience. Pick a built-in crowd or describe yours in a few lines ("ER nurses on night shift", "Warzone squad", "first-year teachers") and the captions get written in their voice.

Can I upload anything?

Only footage you have the rights to use. You keep what you make. See the Terms and the Copyright policy.

What happens to my videos?

They're kept for your plan's retention period, then deleted. You can delete a run, download your data or delete your account whenever you like.
