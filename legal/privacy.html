This explains what {{COMPANY}} ("we") collects when you use {{APP_NAME}}, why, who else sees it, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address, your name if you give one, a securely hashed password (we never store the password itself), when you agreed to our terms, and when you signed in.
  • Your content: the videos you upload or link to, the clips we make from them, the captions, the audiences you create, and your 👍/👎 ratings of captions (used to tune captions for your account).
  • Usage: how many minutes and clips you use, your plan and billing status, and technical records of runs (for example errors and how long they took).
  • Payments: handled by Stripe. We receive your plan, billing status and a customer reference from Stripe. We do not see or store your card number.
  • Technical data: your IP address and browser type in server logs and for security and rate limiting.

We use one cookie, to keep you signed in. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Why we use it

To run the Service: process your videos and make clips, keep your account and plan, take payment, keep the Service secure and prevent abuse, send you account emails (confirming your address, resetting your password, billing notices), and support you.

Who else processes it

We use other companies to run the Service. They receive only what they need:

  • AI providers (Google, Anthropic, OpenAI): your video (a reduced-quality copy), its audio and transcript text, and the captions are sent to them to analyse the video and write and check captions. Their own terms and privacy policies apply to that processing.
  • Cloudflare: stores your uploaded videos and finished clips.
  • Hosting and database providers: run the application and store your account data.
  • Stripe: payments and subscriptions.
  • An email provider: sends our account emails.

We do not sell your personal data.

How long we keep it

Your runs and clips are kept for the number of days your plan includes, then deleted automatically. If your subscription ends, your account is locked and we keep your content so it is there if you return; we will not delete it unless you ask us to, or we give you at least 30 days' notice by email. When you delete your account, we delete your content and personal data, except what we must keep for legal, tax or fraud-prevention reasons (such as payment records).
