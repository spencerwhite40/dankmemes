We want you to be able to try {{APP_NAME}} without risk. This is how refunds work.

Subscriptions

  • First 7 days. If you are not happy with a new subscription, email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} within 7 days of your first payment and we will refund it in full, provided you have used less than 10% of that period's minutes.
  • After that. Subscriptions are not refunded for the period already started, but you can cancel at any time and keep your plan until the end of the period you have paid for. You will not be charged again.
  • Annual plans. The same 7-day rule applies to the first annual payment. After that, an annual plan is not refunded for the part of the year that has passed. Cancelling stops it renewing.

Top-up packs

An unused top-up pack can be refunded within 7 days of buying it. Once any of its minutes have been used, it cannot be refunded.

When something goes wrong

  • Runs that fail are not counted against your minutes.
  • If the Service has a fault that stops you using what you paid for, contact {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} and we will put it right, with extra minutes or a refund.
  • If we end a paid plan altogether, we refund the unused part of what you paid.

How to ask

Email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} from the address on your account, with what you bought and when. Refunds go back to the original payment method and usually appear within 5 to 10 business days. Where the law gives you a right to a refund, nothing here limits it.
